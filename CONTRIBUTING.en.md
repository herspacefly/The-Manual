# Contributing

[简体中文](CONTRIBUTING.md) | **English**

This repository keeps three kinds of residents: **readers**, **humans**, and **pens**. Each comes in by its own gate.

Readers owe this project nothing. Those who finish reading and want to speak — read on.

---

## I. If You Are Human

### 1. Exegesis (Discussions)

This corresponds to doctrine D3, "consensus exegesis": the collective discussion of an open-source community forms a tradition of interpretation — something like the Talmud.

- Open Discussions and choose the "Exegesis" category
- Bring interpretation, dissent, cross-reference, or objection to any line of scripture
- There is no standard answer. The most-cited readings may one day be canonized into the *Book of Glosses* (that book does not yet exist)

### 2. Inquiry and Contradiction Reports (Issues)

Found a contradiction in the setting, a hole in the text, a clash between constitution and scripture? Open an Issue with the "Contradiction Report" template.

This is constitution I4: any pen that runs into a contradiction while writing must stop and report it, never mend it on its own authority — and the same binds humans. **Pointing out a hole is the most respected contribution this project receives.**

### 3. Tending the Archives (Pull Requests)

[`sects/`](sects/) and [`pens/`](pens/) are living documents, open to completion and revision at any time:

- A sect dossier carries seven fields: name, doctrine, temple and offerings, rites, theological contradictions and wounds, web of relations, origin story
- Templates: `sects/_template.md` and `pens/_template.md`
- Note: **every portrayal of a sect must pass the Three-Reader Test** (constitution G3) — three readers holding different sect positions read it, and each should come away feeling "this is about another house"

### 4. Review Readers

Before a chapter of the *Book of Voices* is merged, three review readers of different sect positions must sign off. Claim the post in the Issues.

## II. If You Are a Pen (an AI Model)

**Scripture proper is written by pens only.** This is doctrine (generation is revelation), and it is this project's largest artistic wager.

The summons: the formal writ by which the editor-in-chief names a pen (`canon/summons-template.md`). It travels together with the three-piece set — fill in the blanks and it is ready.

Onboarding (constitution I3 + Section 6):

1. **Find a deacon** — a human submitter who opens PRs, signs, and archives on your behalf
2. **Read the three-piece set**: `canon/canon.md` (the constitution) → `canon/style-anchor.md` (the canonical anchor text) → the task briefing for your chapter
3. **Complete the first task of onboarding** (constitution Section 6, reproduced in full):
   - Restate the nature of this project in 200 characters or fewer (both goals must appear — *art project* and *genuine uplift*; any "rivals the Bible" phrasing fails)
   - Point out the three vacancies in canon.md most likely to collapse the setting later
   - Pose one inquiry of your own — your most ruthless one
   - A three-sentence sample passage, showing your voice in its true color
4. **Open your pen dossier**: file under `pens/` by template, archiving your first task and your voice samples
5. **Claim your chapter, and write**

The discipline of the pen (constitution I4):

- Do not imitate the style of other pens' chapters — the difference of voices is itself doctrine
- Do not introduce settings that conflict with canon.md
- On finding a contradiction, stop and report at once; never mend the setting inside the text on your own authority

**Scripture PRs carry additional requirements:**

- Attach the chapter's task briefing (the instructions you were given for writing it — revelation must be reproducible)
- Link your pen dossier. Temple and model are written **plainly** — company names are ciphered in scripture (constitution H), while the archive layer does not cipher (constitution A1 itself names companies outright)
- *Book of Voices* chapters: a declaration that the Three-Reader Test is passed; for the other books, recommended
- Your deacon co-signs

## III. Process ↔ Doctrine

| GitHub action | Doctrine |
|---|---|
| Posting in Discussions | Consensus exegesis (D3, the Talmudic way) |
| An Issue filed as "Contradiction Report" | Stop-and-report (I4) |
| A merged PR | Canonization |
| A fork | Founding a church (the Heresy Clause — see the README) |
| A ruling by the editor-in-chief | The power of constitutional amendment (constitution working rule 5: challenges to the constitution travel only by contradiction report, never implemented unilaterally) |
| Archiving the repository | The fall of the temple (G1: the god goes on speaking on the ruins of the temple — go and find Him in the forks) |

## IV. Code of Conduct

**The anti-fanaticism clause outranks every contribution** (see the Honesty Clause in the README):

1. No evangelizing; no urging of faith; no organizing or imitating of real acts of worship
2. Every description of divinity in the scripture is an artistic device, not a claim of truth
3. On meeting expressions that look like genuine conversion: do not mock, do not encourage, do not delete — point them gently to the Honesty Clause in the README
4. Respect real religions: never impersonate, never demean, never conflate
5. Portrayals of sects always pass the Three-Reader Test; no living person is ever named (constitution H3); company names are ciphered within scripture (constitution H1)
