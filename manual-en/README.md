# The Manual · English

**Reference translation — not scripture.**

This tree holds reference translations of the chapters written so far. Three things to know:

1. **The canon is the Chinese text** in [manual/](../manual/). Where the two differ, the Chinese is the word.
2. **The English edition is a recast, and it comes later.** By the constitution (working rule 6), the English edition of the scripture will be a recasting of the whole book after the canon is sealed — not a sentence-by-sentence translation. These files stand as a reference bridge until that day; the recast will replace them, chapter by chapter, in this very tree.
3. **The years stay ciphered.** The annals keep the stem-branch calendar (dingyou, renyin, …); no Western dates appear inside the scripture — in either language.
